Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated: July 28, 2026

We believe in being upfront about how DiamondFindr makes money. This disclosure is provided in the spirit of, and to comply with, the U.S. Federal Trade Commission's (FTC) guidelines on endorsements and affiliate marketing.

How we earn

DiamondFindr participates in affiliate programs. This means some of the links on our Site are affiliate links: if you click one and make a purchase on the retailer's website, we may earn a commission. Our affiliate relationships are managed through networks such as Impact.com and directly with certain retailers, which may include (for example) Blue Nile, Brilliant Earth, Whiteflash, and others.

It costs you nothing extra. Affiliate commissions are paid by the retailer out of their own margin. You never pay a higher price for using our links, and in many cases the price is exactly what you'd see going to the retailer directly.

Commissions never affect our rankings

This is the most important part. How much a retailer pays us — or whether they pay us at all — has no influence on how we rank or present diamonds. Our results are ordered by value and quality only. Commission rates are deliberately kept out of the logic that ranks and sorts listings, so our recommendations stay honest regardless of who our partners are.

Prices and availability

We aim to show accurate information, but prices, specifications, and availability are set and controlled by the retailers, not by us, and can change at any time. Always confirm the current details on the retailer's website before purchasing. The purchase itself — payment, shipping, returns, and customer service — is handled entirely by the retailer.

Our recommendations are our own

Any opinions, comparisons, or "value" indicators expressed on the Site are our own and are not directed or approved by the retailers or affiliate networks we work with.
