About DiamondFindr

An independent way to search and compare diamonds across many retailers — ranked by value and quality, never by who pays us.

What we do

DiamondFindr is a free diamond search and comparison tool. We bring together natural and lab-grown diamonds from a range of online retailers so you can filter by the things that actually matter — shape, carat, cut, color, clarity, and price — in one place. When you find a stone you like, we send you straight to the retailer to view the details and buy.

Why we built it

Buying a diamond is confusing. The same specs can carry very different prices from one retailer to the next, the jargon is intimidating, and most tools are cluttered or quietly steer you toward whoever pays them the most. We wanted something simpler and more honest: search in plain English or with a few filters, see clear options, and decide for yourself.

Our promise: unbiased ranking

We rank by value and quality — never by commission. How much a retailer pays us has no influence on how results are ordered or which diamonds you see. This isn't just a policy; it's built into how the site works, so it stays honest as we grow.

How we make money

DiamondFindr is free to use. When you click through to a retailer and make a purchase, we may earn an affiliate commission — at no extra cost to you. That's what keeps the lights on, and it never affects our rankings. You can read the details on our Affiliate Disclosure page.

How it works

Search or filter for the diamond you want, compare the matching options and their prices, then click through to the retailer to complete your purchase on their site. Prices, availability, and the final sale are always handled by the retailer — we're the map, not the store.
